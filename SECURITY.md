# Security policy

This library protects health data, so we take reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Instead:

- use GitHub's **"Report a vulnerability"** (Security → Advisories) on this repository, or
- email **TODO: security contact address** (to be added before publishing)

Include what you found, how to reproduce it and what an attacker could do with it. We'll reply within 5 working days.

## Scope

In scope:
- this repository's code;
- the protocol in SPEC.md, including design flaws in it.

The open questions in SPEC.md §13 are known. Arguments for a particular answer are welcome as normal issues.

Out of scope:
- the Spanda apps and server;
- the limits listed in SPEC.md §1.2.
